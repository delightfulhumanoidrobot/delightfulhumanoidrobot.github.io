Vegan Lemon Chiffon Pie

Recipe insporation: Fig Food Guide

Serves 8 people

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
Crust
Vegan grahm cracker crumbs 1 and 1/2 cups
Organic cane sugar 3 tbsp
Melted vegan butter 5 tbsp
Filling
Aquafaba 3/4 cups
Cream of tartar 1/4 tbsp
Organic cane sugar 2/3 cup
Coconut milk 1 can
Fresh lemon juice from 2 lemons
Lemon zest 1 tbsp
Vanilla extract 1 tsp
Salt 1/4 tsp

Recipe

  1. mv grahm cracker crumbs, 3 tbsp sugar, and melted butter into the medium-bowl directory.
  2. You should see a new file called new-mixture.crust. mv new-mixture.crust into the pie-dish directory.
  3. mv Aquafaba, Cream of tartar, Organic cane sugar, Coconut milk, Fresh lemon juice, Lemon zest, Vanilla extract, the remaining Sugar, and Salt into the medium-bowl directory.
  4. Stir until well-mixed. Then, you should see a new file in the directory named chiffon.filling. mv this file into the pie-dish directory.
  5. Now, place the pie-dish directory in the oven and cook until Chris (me) fixes the LED screen that tells you how much time is left in class.
  6. Take out of the oven and enjoy!
