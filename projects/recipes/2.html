Terminally Tasty Vegan Tacos

Made intierly by me, Chris Jones (absolutley) Serves 1 person. Takes less than 30 minutes to make

About This Recipe

I have developed this recipe over countless years of being vegan. It is my go-to dish whenever I am hungry. It is significant to me because, when I first became vegan, I still wanted to eat tacos, so I developed my own meatless taco recipe.

My vegan tacos are good
(200%) Chris Jones

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
Tortilla bread 2 pieces
Baked beans 1 cup
Avacado 1
Shredded cheese 2 pinches
Lettuce 2 pieces
Sour cream 2 spoons
Salsa 2 spoons

Recipe

  1. mv tortilla-1 and tortilla-2 to the directory clean-counterspace
  2. cd clean-counterspace
  3. vim tortilla-1
  4. Enter 
     INSERT
    mode
  5. Add half your sour cream to tortilla-1
  6. Then, add half your baked beans, avacado, shredded cheese, salsa, and lettuce on top
  7. Esc :wq to wrap tortilla-1
  8. Now, vim tortilla-2 and repeat the process

Tips/Tricks

  • Decide which type of baked beans to use
    • I use black beans, but if you prefer another type, feel free to try it out
  • cd tortilla-1 and tortilla-2 into the toaster a few minutes before making this recipe so that they taste fresh
  • Eat Lemon Chiffon Pie after you have the tacos.
